## ⚠️ HEADLINE FINDING — Sprint 8 closes with 0/8 Must-Have items landed; combat still non-functional in both directions

Sprint 8 was explicitly scoped as a **recovery sprint** with a single goal: land BUG-041/042/043/012

standup, the sprint entered its last scheduled day at **0/8 Must-Have tasks landed**, with zero

`git log sprint-08` still shows the same HEAD (`a29895b`, Fri 08-07 standup commit) as of this run.

This cycle's 3 parallel code-review agents re-verified every claimed bug directly against current code
(not the tracker) and found the picture is **worse than "still open" for two of the three P0s**:

- **Player → Enemy (BUG-041): CONFIRMED STILL FULLY OPEN.** `MeleeWeapon.cs:17-20` `Attack()` is an
  empty override; `MakeDamage()` (lines 21-25) has its body fully commented out and has **zero call
  sites anywhere in the codebase** (repo-wide grep confirmed by the reviewing agent). The full call
  chain was traced end-to-end: `PlayerAttackState.cs:44` → `WeaponHolder.cs:39-43` → only calls
  `Weapon.SetAnimation()`, never `Weapon.Attack()`. Player deals zero melee damage, unchanged from last
  cycle.
- **Enemy → Player (BUG-042/BUG-053): NOT actually fixed — worse than the tracker assumed.** The
  `NotImplementedException` is gone from `EntityCore`, but the live path through
  `EntityNegativeReciver.TakeDamage()` is **dead on arrival for an independent reason not previously
  logged**: `currentHealth` (a `public int`, never initialized from `EntityStatsSO`) defaults to `0`,
  and the guard `if (currentHealth <= 0) return;` makes every call a silent no-op before the
  wrong-hub crash (`Core.GetCoreComponent(out PlayerInputHandler input)` — `Core` is `EntityCore`,
  which has no `PlayerInputHandler`) is even reached. **Filed as BUG-054, extends BUG-053.**
- **BUG-043 (divergent enemy attack paths): CONFIRMED STILL OPEN**, and now with more precision: only
  `EntityAttack.cs` (hardcoded `damage=10`, ignores `AttackSO`) is actually wired — `EntityWeaponMelee.cs`
  (CLAUDE.md's designated "reference implementation," SO-driven) is never invoked; `weaponHolder` is
  cached in `EntityBasicState.cs` but unused.

**Net: this is the second consecutive sprint where the stated recovery goal was not reached, and the
second time BUG-042/053 has been marked "fixed" at a shallow level (exception removed) while the
underlying damage path remains non-functional for a different reason.**
