# BUG-106

**Title**: Weapon and ability bindings are applied only from `Player.SetCharacterData()` — enemies never get their weapon or abilities, and the player is unarmed until a Champion is confirmed
**Severity**: S2 (enemies cannot attack at all — no weapon, no attack since `f7d98b1`; no error, no warning)
**Priority**: 1 (blocks the combat loop of every room; one call site per character type)
**System**: Character / Weapons / Abilities v2
**Found by**: `/daily-standup --auto` 2026-10-13 (NOTE-20261013-3, static read of `51f8d2d4`); confirmed by weekly wrap-up triage 2026-10-19 at `sprint-18` `0def62ab`
**Status**: Open — confirmed by reading code; not yet observed in Play Mode
**Fixed in**: —

## Description

`51f8d2d4` "polish" (2026-10-12) emptied the equip logic out of `Start()`:

| Before | After (`0def62ab`) |
|---|---|
| `AbilityHolderBase.Start()` copied `CharacterData.AbilityBindings` and equipped each slot | `Start()` only calls `base.Start()`; the work moved to a new public `SetAbility()` (`AbilityHolderBase.cs:68-78`) |
| `WeaponHolderBase.Start()` equipped `CharacterData.DefaultWeapon` | `Start()` only resolves `vital` / `input`; the work moved to a public `EquipDefaultWeapon()` (`WeaponHolderBase.cs:45-54`) |

The only caller of either method is `Player.SetCharacterData()` (`Player.cs:81-92`, marked
"Need check and fix this logic late"). `git grep` at `0def62ab` finds no other call to `SetAbility()`
or `EquipDefaultWeapon()`.

Consequences:

1. **Enemies.** `Entity.SetCharacterData()` (`Entity.cs:78-82`) only swaps `data` and renames the
   GameObject, and pooled enemies are never sent through it anyway. So every enemy spawns with
   `weapon == null` (every `WeaponHolderBase` attack entry point returns false) and an empty
   `_equipped` dictionary (no `EntityAbilityState` cast). Enemies chase but never attack.
2. **Player.** Until a Champion is confirmed in the start room the player has no weapon and no
   abilities; the HUD hotbar binds on `ON_PLAYER_READY` before any are equipped.

Relates to NOTE-20261009-6 (Needs owner: what a Champion pick should re-seed) and BUG-107 (the
equip method itself throws on a re-equip).

## Expected fix (owner)

Equip the defaults once on spawn for both sides (e.g. from `CharacterBase` after injection, or from
`Start()` as before), and let `SetCharacterData()` re-equip on a Champion pick. Pooled enemies keep
their weapon across re-spawns, so the spawn path must not duplicate it.

## How to verify

Play Mode: enter a combat room and stand still next to an enemy — it must attack and the player HP
bar must drop. Before the Champion pick, LMB must swing the default weapon.
