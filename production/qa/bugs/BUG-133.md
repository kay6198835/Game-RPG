# BUG-133

**Title**: New hit knockback pushes the victim toward the attacker — `CharacterInputBase.OnTakeDamage()` passes `hitDirectionVector` (attacker minus victim) as the knockback velocity
**Severity**: S4 (small today: unit speed for 0.1 s ≈ 0.1 world units; wrong direction and movement is locked meanwhile)
**Priority**: 3 (one sign; tune the strength in the same change)
**System**: Character base (input / movement), combat feel
**Found by**: weekly wrap-up 2026-11-20, review of the week's `.cs` (inbox note NOTE-20261120-8), STATIC at `sprint-23` `0069a40c`
**Status**: Open — confirmed by reading code; not observed in Play Mode
**Fixed in**: —

## Description

`19540dff` (2026-11-20) added to `Assets/Script/Character/Base/CharacterInputBase.cs:44-45`:

```
hitDirectionVector = (attackPosition - (Vector2)transform.position).normalized;
movement?.ApplyKnockback(hitDirectionVector, GameConstants.SettingStats.TAKE_DAMAGE_FLAG_DURATION);
```

`hitDirectionVector` points from the victim **to** the attacker (it is the facing used by the
take-damage animation). `MovementBase.FixedUpdate()` (`MovementBase.cs:47-52`) moves the body by
`knockbackVelocity * fixedDeltaTime`, so the victim is pulled toward the attacker, at 1 unit/s for
`TAKE_DAMAGE_FLAG_DURATION` (0.1 s). `CanMove` is false while knocked back.

## Fix direction (owner)

Use `-hitDirectionVector * knockbackStrength` (strength from data, not a literal).

## How to verify

Play Mode: hit an enemy from its left — it moves slightly to the right, not into the player.
