## Description

`221d54be` added `UIEvents.OnOpenConfirmPanel(Action callback, ConfirnData data)`
(`Assets/Script/UIFlow/Core/UIEvents.cs:55-59`), called by `ChampitionController.Interact()`
(`Assets/Script/System/StartGameSystem/ChampitionController.cs:27-31`).

1. `:57` raises `OpenConfirnPanel.Invoke(data)` with no null-conditional. Its only subscriber is
   `GameplayUIController.OnEnable()` (`GameplayUIController.cs:37`), which lives in the additive
   `GameplayUI` scene. Entering Play Mode directly on `LoadRandomMap` (the documented main dev scene)
   and interacting with a champion monument throws `NullReferenceException`. The other raisers on the
   same bus use `?.Invoke` (`UIEvents.cs:36,38,43`). Review-flow check R3.6.
2. `AccessAction()` (`:61-65`) raises `ConfirnRequest.Invoke()` unguarded as well.
3. `ConfirnPanel.SetConfirm()` (`ConfirnPanel.cs:17-21`) assigns `tileText.text = data.confirmText` and
   `messText.text = data.cancelText`, so the title shows "Xác nhận" and the message shows "Hủy"; the
   question is never shown and the buttons keep their scene labels.
4. *(wrap-up addition)* `UIEvents.ResetStatics()` (`:46-53`) clears the five older events but not
   `OpenConfirnPanel` or `ConfirnRequest`. With domain reload disabled, a stale subscriber and a stale
   callback (holding the previous session's `ChampitionController` and player) survive into the next
   Play Mode session.

## Reproduction (expected)

1. Open `Assets/Scenes/Main/LoadRandomMap.unity` alone, enter Play Mode.
2. Walk to a champion monument in the first room and press Interact (G).
3. Expected: confirm panel. Actual (by reading): `NullReferenceException` at `UIEvents.cs:57`.

## Fix direction

- `OpenConfirnPanel?.Invoke(data)`; `ConfirnRequest?.Invoke()`.
- Clear both new events in `ResetStatics()`.
- Give `ConfirnData` title/message fields and map them to `tileText`/`messText`; set button labels
  through the two `Button` children.
- Move the strings out of `ChampitionController` (`.claude/rules/ui-code.md` "Localization Ready").
