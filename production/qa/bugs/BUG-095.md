# BUG-095

**Title**: `Arrow.prefab` has no projectile script or collider — ranged attacks spawn inert sprites that never move, hit or despawn
**Severity**: S2 (feature does nothing; no error, no log)
**Priority**: 1 (blocks S16-05 / S16-07 ranged-weapon verification)
**System**: Weapons / RangeWeapon (content)
**Found by**: Weekly wrap-up code review, 2026-09-28 (branch `sprint-16`, HEAD `5b035b7` "range weapon")
**Status**: Open — confirmed by reading the prefab YAML

## Description

`5b035b7` added `Assets/Prefab/Particle Effect/Attack Projectile/Arrow.prefab` and the three
`Range Attack SO State 1-3.asset` stages that spawn it through `RangeWeapon.OnActivate()`.

The prefab contains only four GameObjects (`Arrow`, `GameObject`, `Square`, `Triangle`), their
`Transform`s and two `SpriteRenderer`s (`--- !u!212`). There is **no `MonoBehaviour`** (no `bullet`,
`Projectile` or `PoolMember`), **no `Collider2D`** and **no `Rigidbody2D`**.

Separately, `RangeWeapon.OnActivate(float finalDamage)` (`:40-59`) never uses `finalDamage` — the
stat-scaled damage computed by `WeaponHolderBase.CalculateCurrentDamage()` is dropped. Even with a
working projectile script, damage would come only from `BulletDataSO`, unlike melee.

## Consequence

- Each shot spawns `ProjectileCount` static sprites at the fire point. They do not move, deal no damage
  and are never released, so `ObjectPoolManager` instantiates new ones on every shot (unbounded growth).
- Combined with BUG-094, the ranged weapon is non-functional end to end even though BUG-064 item 7
  (the DI wiring) is now code-complete.

## Fix

1. Add the project's projectile component (`bullet` + `BulletDataSO`, the same one the old bullet
   prefab used), a `Collider2D` (trigger) and a `Rigidbody2D` to `Arrow.prefab`, or point the stages
   at the existing bullet prefab.
2. Pass `finalDamage` into the spawned projectile (or record an explicit decision that ranged damage is
   `BulletDataSO`-only).

## Verify

Play Mode: fire at an enemy; the arrow must travel, reduce enemy HP and return to the pool
(hierarchy count under the pool must not grow shot after shot).
