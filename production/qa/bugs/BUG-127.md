# BUG-127

**Title**: Mage basic attack projectile speed is `1` — a MagicBolt travels at most 4 units and needs 2.5 s to reach a target 2.5 units away
**Severity**: S3 (the Mage's basic attack is close to useless; no exception)
**Priority**: 2 (data only — three asset fields)
**System**: Weapons / data (`Assets/SO/Weapons/MageWeapon/`)
**Found by**: automated QA run `20261114-2329` / `20261114-2357`, cases QA-CMB-04.Mage and QA-CMB-05.Mage (inbox note NOTE-20261115-3), re-read at `sprint-22` `ff7f3ecb` by the 2026-11-15 wrap-up
**Status**: Open — confirmed at runtime by the automated QA run (2 of 2 runs) and by reading the asset
**Fixed in**: —

## Description

`Assets/SO/Weapons/MageWeapon/Mage Attack SO State 1.asset` (added in `0f3644bd`, 2026-11-08) stores
`ProjectileConfig.speed: 1`, `lifetime: 4`, `attackRange: 10`; States 2 and 3 follow the same shape
per the QA note. At speed 1 a bolt needs 2.5 s for a target 2.5 units away and expires after 4 units,
well short of the authored `attackRange` of 10.

QA-CMB-04.Mage: no bolt reached the target within 2.5 s, one bolt still active 2 s later.
QA-CMB-05.Mage: 0 shots landed in 2 s of clicking. The run reported both as KNOWN under BUG-095 /
BUG-103 (Archer `Arrow.prefab`), which does not apply: `MagicBolt.prefab` has a trigger
`CircleCollider2D`. The QA side narrowed the BUG-095 mapping to the Archer.

## Expected fix (owner)

Author a speed and lifetime that cover `attackRange` (for example speed 10, lifetime 1) on all three
Mage stages, or derive lifetime from `attackRange / speed`.

## How to verify

QA-CMB-04.Mage passes; in Play Mode a Mage bolt reaches an enemy a few units away within a fraction
of a second and deals damage.

## Related

BUG-095 (Archer arrow), BUG-103 (`RecoveryTime` unused).
